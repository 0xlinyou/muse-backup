Privacy Policy – Muse Backup

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What this app is

Muse Backup is a personal backup tool for my own use. It backs up the core files of my Muse assistant to my own Google Drive once a day.

What Google data it accesses

The app uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope. It can only access the files it creates in my Google Drive – the daily backup snapshots in the folder “Muse 备份”. It does not read, list or open any other files in my Drive.

How the data is used and stored

The snapshot files are stored only in my own Google Drive. They are not sent to any other server, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising or tracking.

Data sharing

No Google user data is shared with any third party.

Deleting your data

Delete the snapshot files from the “Muse 备份” folder in Google Drive. You can also revoke the app’s access at any time in your Google Account settings under “Third-party access”.
